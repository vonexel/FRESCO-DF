# Matched PD and ALE response comparison

All ten frozen teachers per head; 564457 common-support pairs. Negative ALE−PD G favors ALE. Both methods use the same source-train background and identical held-out pairs and equal-video weights.

See metrics.json for paired fixed-seed cluster and crossed seed × cluster percentile intervals. Zero-change prediction is G=1 where the denominator is defined.

## Limitations

Exploratory after target inspection; intervals are unadjusted and conditional on the observed source data and generators. PD/ALE describe model responses, not causal effects; marginal support does not ensure joint-manifold support. The original PD and G_curve remain unchanged.
