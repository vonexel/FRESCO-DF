# Response: common supported pairs

All ten original seeds per head; 121493 common-support frame/condition pairs. G_curve, G_block and zero use identical pairs and equal-video weights. The historical full-test G_block belongs to a different population and is not subtracted here.

See metrics.json for approximate fixed-seed cluster and crossed seed × cluster percentile CIs, metrics.csv for condition-level response metrics, and figures/ for SVG and source CSV.

## Limitations

Exploratory after prior target inspection; intervals are unadjusted and conditional on the source training data and test generators. Source marginal support and Mahalanobis distance do not establish joint manifold support. Hybrid responses are not causal effects. Component energies need not sum to one because of covariance. Near-zero responses are counted.
